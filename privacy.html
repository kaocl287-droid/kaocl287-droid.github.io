AIOS Privacy Policy

AIOS is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

  • It accesses only the owner's own accounts (such as Google and QuickBooks), connected by the owner, to show the owner their own information (for example, what arrived in their inbox).
  • Data read from Google stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No other people's accounts are accessed, and there are no other users.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the connected service's account settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
